## 📌 Problem Statement

Hostel wardens currently manage operations like attendance, gate passes, visitor logs, and mess feedback using **manual registers and WhatsApp groups**.

This leads to:

* ❌ Lack of transparency
* ❌ Delayed approvals
* ❌ Poor record management
* ❌ Communication gaps

---

## 💡 Solution

**HostelSync** is a unified digital platform designed to streamline hostel operations by replacing manual processes with an **automated, real-time system**.

It provides:

* Centralized management dashboard
* Real-time updates and tracking
* Efficient communication between students, wardens, and parents

---

## ⚙️ Key Features

### 🚪 Digital Gate Pass Workflow

* Students request gate passes online
* Wardens can approve/reject instantly
* Real-time status tracking

---

### 📩 Parent SMS Notifications

* Automatic alerts sent to parents
* Improves student safety and transparency

---

### 🏠 Real-Time Room Management

* Track room allocations
* Monitor occupancy
* Manage student records efficiently

---

### 🧾 Complaint Management with SLA

* Raise complaints digitally
* Track resolution with SLA (Service Level Agreement)
* Priority-based issue handling

---

### 🍽️ Mess Menu Voting

* Students vote for preferred meals
* Collect feedback digitally
* Improve mess quality using data

---

### 👥 Visitor Log System

* Digital record of visitors
* Easy tracking and management

---
